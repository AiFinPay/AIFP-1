## Status Notice

This informational proposal is **not part of the current AIFP-1 economic profile**. Its previous dynamic min/max ranges and reputation-discount assumptions were based on superseded pricing and MUST NOT be used as current product guidance.

Current AIFP-1 reference action tiers are **gross payer prices**:

| Tier | Gross Reference Price | Merchant 99% | AiFinPay 1% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `standard` | $0.0005 | $0.000495 | $0.000005 |
| `complex` | $0.002 | $0.00198 | $0.00002 |
| `premium` | $0.005 | $0.00495 | $0.00005 |

Current AIFP-1 merchant-monetization economics are gross-inclusive `treasuryBps=100`, `creator/referralBps=0`: the payer settles the gross quoted amount, the merchant receives 99% of gross, AiFinPay receives 1% of gross, and the 1% is not added on top. AIFP-2/x402 is a separate `0/0` profile.

## Historical Abstract

This proposal originally recommended clamping dynamic prices to merchant-defined bounds and applying reputation-based discounts. Those rules were never promoted into the current canonical AIFP-1 pricing profile.

## Migration Guidance

Implementations MUST NOT infer dynamic price bounds, reputation discounts, or custom URL pricing from this AIP. Any future dynamic-pricing mechanism requires a new or explicitly reactivated proposal with current economics, abuse controls, deterministic quote semantics, and merchant-facing configuration requirements.

## Backward Compatibility

Historical implementations may retain old behavior for audit/replay purposes, but it must be labeled legacy and isolated from current AIFP-1 quotes.

## Security Considerations

Future reputation-based pricing must address manipulation, sybil behavior, quote determinism, replay, and policy bypass before activation.
