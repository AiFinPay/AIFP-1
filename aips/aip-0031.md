## Abstract

Recommends clamping dynamic prices to `[min, max]` and capping reputation-based discounts at −30% to prevent gaming.

## Motivation

Provide guidance for merchants implementing the Dynamic Pricing Engine to prevent abuse while rewarding good reputation.

## Specification

### Price Bounds

Dynamic prices MUST be clamped to `[min_price, max_price]` per pricing tier:

| Tier | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|
| `standard` | $0.00001 | $0.00010 |
| `complex` | $0.00006 | $0.00050 |
| `premium` | $0.00010 | $0.00100 |

### Reputation Discount

- Maximum discount: −30%
- Applies to agents with reputation ≥ 700
- Linear scaling from 500 (0%) to 700 (−30%)

### Anti-Gaming Measures

- Rate-limit reputation changes
- Monitor for suspicious patterns
- Merchants MAY override discounts

## Backward Compatibility

Non-binding guidance; no protocol changes required.

## Security Considerations

Prevents reputation manipulation for price exploitation.
