## Abstract

Ratifies the fixed AIFP-1 per-action pricing schedule and removes protocol-level
dynamic pricing for the three standard tiers.

## Motivation

A single public schedule makes merchant configuration, agent budgeting,
receipts, analytics, and commercial forecasts consistent across the protocol.

## Specification

| Tier | Fixed Price Per Action |
|---|---:|
| `standard` | USD 0.0005 |
| `complex` | USD 0.002 |
| `premium` | USD 0.005 |

The protocol fee remains 1% of each successful transaction. Merchant-funded
free quotas remain configurable and do not change the price of paid actions.

Merchants may sell separate products such as subscriptions, premium content,
API packages, or enterprise access at merchant-defined prices. Those products
must be recorded separately from the fixed AIFP-1 action tiers.

## Backward Compatibility

Implementations using the earlier schedule must migrate configured tier values,
examples, schemas, and quote validation to this schedule. Receipts issued before
migration remain valid until their normal expiry.

## Security Considerations

Agents must compare amounts using decimal or integer arithmetic and enforce
their configured per-call and aggregate budget limits before payment.
